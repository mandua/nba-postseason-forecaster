Selection

Why Logistic Regression

Logistic Regression was selected because it produced the best Log Loss and Brier Score. That matters here because the probabilities feed the postseason simulation. The final model was then retrained using all games through 2025-26.

Methodology

What Powers the Forecast

  • 9,509 NBA regular-season games
  • Seasons 2018-19 through 2025-26
  • 84 leakage-safe pregame features
  • Last-5, last-10, and last-20 rolling statistics
  • Season-to-date features
  • Rest and back-to-back context
  • Scaled Logistic Regression
  • Official 2026-27 schedule data for scheduled-game predictions
  • Projected records based on team-strength estimates rather than full schedule simulation
  • 10,000-run Monte Carlo postseason simulation
  • 2-2-1-1-1 best-of-seven home-court format

Limitations

How to Read the Projections

  • The official 2026-27 schedule powers scheduled-game lookup and prediction, including dates, times, arenas, locations, and home/away teams.
  • Season record projections are team-strength estimates, not a full game-by-game simulation of that schedule.
  • Injuries are not directly modeled.
  • Every 2026 offseason roster transaction is not fully modeled.
  • Projections are probabilistic.
  • Historical performance does not guarantee future outcomes.
